# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, and Motion. The repository is prepared for Cloudflare Pages, but deployment remains outside the initial build.

## Users

- Early-stage founders who need to validate, design, and ship an MVP quickly.
- Small businesses that need a credible website or a modern replacement for an outdated one.
- Existing product teams with an AI-generated or partially built prototype that needs production engineering, infrastructure, reliability, or launch support.

## Product Purpose

PDT Labs is the client-services and product-studio brand connected to PriyanshDoesTech. The website should convert qualified visitors into project inquiries while showing that the studio has already shipped and operated real products.

## Positioning

PDT Labs combines senior production engineering judgment with fast, hands-on product delivery. The studio does not stop at prototypes. It can shape, build, harden, and launch web, mobile, AI-assisted, and infrastructure-heavy products.

## Operating Context

Prospects will usually arrive from YouTube, LinkedIn, GitHub, direct outreach, or referrals. They need to understand the offer, expected timeline, indicative investment, proof of execution, and next action within a few minutes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer work in concrete timelines expressed in days or weeks.
- Cover product strategy, landing pages, MVPs, web applications, Android products, AI integrations, backend systems, infrastructure, production readiness, analytics, and launch support.
- Use real project imagery and concise case-study summaries.
- Do not use Priyansh's photograph.
- Do not publish Priyansh's city, home location, or other unnecessary personal-location details.
- Keep the existing job-application portfolio separate.
- Keep the initial repository public.
- Do not deploy the website during the initial build.
- Exact service scope is confirmed during an inquiry. Published prices are starting points rather than fixed quotes.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: PDT Labs.
- PDT expands to PriyanshDoesTech.
- The site should feel polished, technical, fast, and founder-friendly.
- Use a visible top navigation.
- Visual references include 2048 Labs and the two supplied creative portfolio boards, but the implementation must remain original.
- Avoid fabricated testimonials, customer logos, success rates, and delivery statistics.

## Evidence on Hand

- Nest: offline-first Android expense tracker with SMS import and private on-device categorization.
- Dictate: free, open-source, local-first macOS voice-to-text alternative.
- TriviaHub: browser trivia and games product with active early traffic.
- AnimeExplore: anime discovery and information site with early organic search traffic.
- Betternote: local-first macOS notes app built with Rust, React, TypeScript, and SQLite.
- Resumate: AI-assisted cover-letter builder with resume parsing, authentication, rate limiting, live editing, and PDF export.
- Public code and live product pages are available for the featured work.
- No client testimonials, customer logos, or client revenue claims have been supplied and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show shipped work before making broad claims.
2. Make scope, timeline, and next steps easy to understand.
3. Present speed as disciplined execution, not rushed development.
4. Keep the relationship personal and senior-led while presenting PDT Labs as a durable studio brand.
5. Prefer a small number of convincing projects over a long undifferentiated portfolio.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive alternative text, and responsive layouts from small phones through large desktop screens.
